Villa Policies & House Rules

Transparency first. These policies apply to all 5 Massarossa villas and match our listings on Booking.com, Agoda and Airbnb. Please read before booking.

💰 Additional Charges

⚠️ Please note: the nightly rate does NOT include electricity consumption and (optionally) deep cleaning. Details below — no surprises at check-out.

1. Cleaning

  • Standard final cleaning: included in the nightly rate
  • Deep cleaning (on request): ฿1,000–1,500 depending on villa size
  • Excessive mess surcharge: ฿500–1,000 if extra cleaning is required after check-out

2. Electricity

  • Rate: ฿6 per kWh, metered (staff reads the meter at check-in and check-out)
  • Typical consumption: ฿200–500 per day depending on AC usage
  • Payment: at check-out, cash (THB) or added to the final invoice
💡 Tip: switch off AC and lights when leaving the villa — most guests pay less than ฿300/day.

3. Extra guests & pets

  • Extra guest above max occupancy: ฿500 per person per night
  • Pets: ฿500 per pet per night · max 2 pets · up to 10 kg each
  • Crib / extra bed: ฿500 per stay (on request, subject to availability)
